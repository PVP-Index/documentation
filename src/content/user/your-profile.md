# Your profile

Your player profile is your home on PvPIndex. URL:

```
pvpindex.com/players/<your-minecraft-username>
```

The username in the URL is your **Minecraft** username, not your website account username. The two are independent.

## Sections of the profile

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | Avatar, username, ELO across game modes, claim status. |
| **Rankings** | Per-game-mode current ELO, rank, win/loss. Sortable. |
| **ELO history** | A line chart of your ELO in the selected game mode over time. |
| **Recent battles** | Latest 25 battles. Click any row for the full battle page. |
| **Season standings** | Final rank in each past season you participated in. |
| **Achievements** | Cosmetic flair earned over time (e.g. "First win", "10-win streak"). |

## Claimed vs unclaimed

If your profile is unclaimed, the header shows a **Claim this profile** button to anyone logged in. See [Claiming your Minecraft account](/user/claiming-your-minecraft-account).

If it's claimed, only **you** (the linked user) can edit profile-level settings.

## Editing your profile

Once claimed, an **Edit profile** button appears in the header. From there you can set:

- A short bio (markdown, 280-character limit).
- Social links (Twitter/X, YouTube, Twitch).
- Your preferred game mode (used to pick the default chart on your profile).
- Your profile-card theme (a few accent colour presets).

## Privacy controls

Under **Account settings → Privacy** you can:

- Hide your real-time ELO from the public leaderboard. Past season snapshots remain visible.
- Hide your battle history from logged-out visitors.
- Disable forum DMs.

Hiding does **not** retroactively remove your data — just controls who can see it.

## Sharing your profile

Every profile is open-graph-tagged, so dropping a profile URL into Discord, Twitter, etc. produces a rich preview with your username, ELO, and current rank.
