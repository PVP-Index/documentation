# Creating an account

You don't need an account to view leaderboards, battles, or player profiles on PvPIndex — everything public is, well, public. You **do** need an account to:

- Claim your Minecraft player profile (so it links to "you" instead of just a UUID).
- Comment on disputed battles.
- Post on the forum.

## Signing up

1. Visit [pvpindex.com/register](https://pvpindex.com/register).
2. Pick a username, an email address, and a password.
3. Submit. You're logged in immediately — no email verification step right now.

The username you pick on the website is **independent** of your Minecraft username. You can claim a Minecraft profile to link the two.

## Choosing a username

- 3–24 characters
- letters, digits, `_` and `-`
- case-preserved (display) but case-insensitive (login)

Usernames are unique. If the one you want is taken, the form will tell you immediately.

## Logging in

Visit [pvpindex.com/login](https://pvpindex.com/login). You can log in with **either** your username **or** your email address.

## Forgot your password?

Hit the **Forgot password** link on the login form. A reset link is emailed to the address on file. Links expire after 60 minutes.

## Changing your email or password

Once logged in, click your avatar (top right) → **Account settings**. Email changes do not currently require re-verification — pick a mailbox you actually own.

## Deleting your account

There is no self-serve delete button right now. Email `support@pvpindex.com` from the address on the account and an admin will delete it within a few business days.

When deleted:

- Your **website account** is removed.
- Your **player profile** stays. Battles you've fought are part of the public record. The profile is unlinked from the user account.
